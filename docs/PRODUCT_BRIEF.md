# Capsule Wardrobe Studio — product brief

## Product statement

Capsule Wardrobe Studio helps people organise the clothes they own and assemble suitable outfits without searching through their entire wardrobe.

## First user

A style-conscious person who owns good clothes but forgets pieces, repeats familiar combinations, and wants to prepare outfits more intentionally.

## First release

A user can:

1. Add a garment with a name, category, colour, season, and optional image.
2. Browse and filter their wardrobe.
3. Select garments to assemble an outfit.
4. Name and save the outfit.

## Not in the first release

- AI outfit recommendations
- Shopping or affiliate links
- Social following
- Weather integrations
- Native mobile applications
- Sophisticated image background removal

## Success criteria

A new user can add one garment and save their first outfit without instructions.

## Design principles

1. **Quiet confidence:** the garments are the focus, not the interface.
2. **One clear next action:** each screen has an obvious priority.
3. **Useful in every state:** empty, loading, error, and success states are designed.
4. **Accessible by default:** keyboard, focus, contrast, labels, and reduced motion are requirements.
5. **Explain decisions:** the portfolio case study records why the product works this way.
